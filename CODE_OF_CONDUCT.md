# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a welcoming environment:

- Being kind, and assuming the other person is acting in good faith.
- Being curious rather than dismissive, especially when a claim about the
  data is challenged.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback. This project cares
  most about being *correct*, and someone who found a real bug deserves
  credit for it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
also when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a GitHub issue or
directly. Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy and security of the reporter is respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be temporarily or
permanently removed from the project.
